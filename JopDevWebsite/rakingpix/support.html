RakingPix Support

Need help with RakingPix? Start with the common topics below.

Scanning a webpage

Open RakingPix on the webpage you want to scan, then choose Scan Page. RakingPix will look for downloadable images on the active page.

Downloading images

Select the images you want, then choose Download Selected. You can also use filters and the Expanded Gallery to make larger groups of images easier to review.

RakingPix Pro

RakingPix Pro adds advanced file naming and folder organization features. Pro access is associated with your RakingPix account after a successful purchase.

Account help

If you cannot sign in, make sure your email has been verified. You can also use the password reset option from the RakingPix sign-in screen.

Something not working?

When reporting a problem, include what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and your Chrome version if possible.

Contact

Support email: support@jopdev.com

Please do not send passwords, payment card details, license secrets, or other sensitive information in a support request.
