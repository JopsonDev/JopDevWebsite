RakingPix Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Overview

RakingPix is a Chrome extension developed by JopDev that helps users find, filter, organize, rename, and download images from the webpage they are currently viewing.

Information RakingPix processes

RakingPix processes information from the active webpage when you choose to scan it. This may include the page URL, page title, domain, image URLs, image dimensions, image metadata, source type, and alt text.

This webpage information is used to provide the extension's image scanning, filtering, preview, organization, naming, and downloading features.

Account information

If you create a RakingPix account, we process your email address and authentication information so that you can sign in, verify your email, reset your password, and access account-based features such as RakingPix Pro.

Passwords are not stored in plaintext. Password processing occurs on the RakingPix backend and only derived password credentials are stored.

Google sign-in

RakingPix may optionally allow you to sign in using Google. Google sign-in is only initiated when you explicitly choose that option.

Payments

RakingPix Pro purchases are processed through Stripe. Stripe processes payment card information. RakingPix does not store your full payment card number.

RakingPix may store transaction and entitlement information needed to determine whether your account has access to Pro features.

Email delivery

RakingPix uses Resend to deliver transactional emails such as account verification and password reset messages.

Local extension data

RakingPix stores certain information locally in Chrome, including preferences, filters, selections, naming and folder settings, tutorial state, theme settings, scan sessions, authentication state, and cached entitlement information.

Analytics and tracking

RakingPix version 0.1.0 does not include custom analytics or telemetry. RakingPix does not maintain a historical log of your browsing activity.

Advertising

RakingPix version 0.1.0 does not currently use an external advertising or tracking provider.

Third-party services

RakingPix relies on service providers including Cloudflare, Stripe, Resend, and Google where applicable. These services process information as necessary to provide backend hosting, payments, transactional email, and optional Google authentication.

Data selling

JopDev does not sell RakingPix user data.

Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect account, authentication, and entitlement information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as RakingPix changes. The updated date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact JopDev through the RakingPix support page.
